How to Play

Goal

Reach the sky-lantern beacon 🚩 at the end of each level. Grab star shards 🪙, find the hidden aether relic ★, touch checkpoints 🏁 so you respawn closer, and stomp or starbolt the critters.

Controls

A/← or left stickMove left
D/→ or left stickMove right
W/↑/Space or A buttonJump — hold for higher
S/↓Crouch / fast-drop through one-ways
Shift or RTRun (faster + longer jumps)
E/J or XFire starbolt (with power-up)
Esc/P or StartPause

Remap keys in Settings. Touch buttons appear on mobile / when enabled.

Critters

Muddle walks · Zipzap sprints · Flutterby flies sine waves · Boingwart hops · Shellyhorn armored (hit only with starbolt or when flashing) · Spitpea lobs seeds · Ticktock patrols platforms. Stomp = bounce kill (+100). Starbolt = +150.

Platforms & hazards

Static · moving · crumbly (fall) · breakable · bouncy 🍄 · hidden ❓ (bump from below). Hazards: spikes, lava, icicles, poison tide, lightning, void. Shields block one hit; falling in pits costs a life.

Power-ups

🍓 Heartberry +1 HP · 🛡 Aether Shield blocks a hit · 🍃 Swiftleaf speed · 🍄 Springshroom mega-jump · ✦ Starbolt shoot. Secrets & fast clears boost score.
