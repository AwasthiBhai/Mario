Meet Pip 🦊 & Nyx ⚔

Pip is the last lantern-fox of Ember Meadow. When the Voidstar swallowed the sky-lanterns, Pip lit one small ember and set out across the Aether to rekindle all 50 beacons.

Nyx is a rift-warden of the Shattered Span. When the Rift tore six worlds open, Nyx forged ember-blades and stormcasters to fell 8 warlords and seal it shut.

  • Pip: run · bounce · starbolt · shield — brave, bouncy, snack-motivated
  • Nyx: slash · dash · volley · wardburst — calm, relentless, scarred

The story

When the Voidstar devoured the sky-lanterns, the worlds of the Aether went dim. Pip, last lantern-fox of Ember Meadow, lit one stubborn ember and set out to rekindle 50 beacons across 10 worlds — from Dewdrop Caverns to the Starfall Citadel.

BundLal Studios

BundLal Studios is an indie micro-studio crafting cozy-but-challenging browser adventures. STARBOUND is our love letter to classic platformers — rebuilt from scratch with original characters, generative music, and a full 50-level arc.
